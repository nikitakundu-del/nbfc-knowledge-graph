# Metric - AIRO Conversation Initiation Share

Layer: Metrics

Confidence: Trusted.

## Definition

Current-stage distinct anonymized funnel users divided by distinct users at the Conversations initiated stage. Funnel-stage and stage-order filters are removed for the denominator; date and other non-stage report filters remain.

## Canonical Names

The semantic-model measures `% of conversations initiated` and `Conversion from conversations initiated` have exactly the same definition. They are aliases of this one canonical metric, not separate metrics.
