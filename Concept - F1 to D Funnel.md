# Concept - F1 to D Funnel

Type: business concept

## Definition

The F1-to-D funnel tracks the customer journey from Form 1 / basic details through downstream financing milestones such as banking, credit assessment, FCU, TNC, agreement, disbursal, token, delivery, and asset events.
